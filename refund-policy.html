RESIST — Refund Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026
Developed by CoreForge

1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for RESIST Pro and other paid features in the RESIST mobile app. How you bought access determines who processes refunds.

This policy supplements our Terms of Service. If anything here conflicts with mandatory consumer protection law in your country, that law applies.

2. Products and pricing

RESIST Pro may be offered as:

  • Monthly subscription — billed every month (price shown at checkout in your local currency).
  • Annual subscription — billed once per year (price shown at checkout).
  • Lifetime access — one-time purchase (price shown at checkout).

Exact prices appear in the Apple App Store, Google Play, or web checkout before you pay. CoreForge may change prices for new purchases with reasonable notice; existing subscriptions follow the store or Paddle renewal rules that apply to your plan.

3. Purchases through Google Play

If you subscribed or paid through Google Play, billing and refunds are handled by Google under Google Play Terms and Google’s refund policies.

  • Request a refund in Google Play: Google Play refund help.
  • Cancel auto-renewal: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RESIST → Cancel.

CoreForge cannot issue refunds for Google Play charges. We can help verify whether RESIST Pro is active on your account after a valid purchase.

4. Purchases through the Apple App Store

If you subscribed or paid through the App Store, billing and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple Media Services Terms and Apple’s policies.

  • Request a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Support.
  • Cancel auto-renewal: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → RESIST → Cancel.

CoreForge cannot issue refunds for Apple charges. We can help troubleshoot Pro access after Apple confirms a valid purchase.

5. Purchases on the web (Paddle)

Some customers buy RESIST Pro through our web checkout, which may be processed by Paddle as merchant of record. In that case, payment and tax receipts come from Paddle, and certain refund requests may be handled by Paddle in line with Paddle’s buyer terms.

Contact us first at hello@resistapp.co within 14 days of purchase if:

  • You were charged but RESIST Pro did not activate after you followed our activation steps;
  • You were charged twice for the same plan;
  • You bought by mistake and have not used RESIST Pro (we may ask for the transaction email or order reference).

We will review eligible cases within 7 business days and, where appropriate, work with Paddle to refund or resolve the charge. Paddle may refuse refunds for digital goods that have been fully delivered and used, except where the law requires otherwise.

Web subscriptions renew according to the plan you selected until you cancel through the manage-subscription link in your Paddle receipt or by emailing hello@resistapp.co. Canceling stops future charges; it does not automatically refund the current billing period unless a refund is granted under this policy or applicable law.

6. Free trials and introductory offers

If a free trial or introductory price is offered, the store or Paddle checkout will show when the trial ends and what you will be charged. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged, using the same subscription settings as in sections 3, 4, or 5 above.

7. No partial refunds for unused time

Except where required by law or explicitly granted under section 5, we do not provide prorated refunds for unused days in a subscription period after cancellation. After cancellation, you keep access until the end of the paid period (store and Paddle rules apply).

8. Lifetime purchases

Lifetime access is a one-time payment for ongoing use of RESIST Pro features available at the time of purchase, for as long as we operate the App and your entitlement remains valid under the Terms of Service.

For web (Paddle) lifetime purchases, contact hello@resistapp.co within 14 days if Pro did not activate or you meet the eligibility in section 5. Store lifetime purchases follow Apple or Google refund policies.

9. EU / UK consumers

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you may have statutory rights including a 14-day right of withdrawal for certain distance contracts. For digital content supplied immediately after purchase, that right may not apply once you begin use and expressly consent to immediate supply, as explained at checkout where required.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights you cannot waive. You may also use any dispute or complaint channels required in your country.

10. How to get help

Email hello@resistapp.co with:

  • The email used for purchase (if any)
  • Whether you paid via Apple, Google Play, or web/Paddle
  • Order or transaction ID, and a short description of the issue

We aim to reply within 7 business days.

11. Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of paid features after an update means you accept the revised policy for future purchases; past purchases are handled under the policy in effect when you paid, subject to mandatory law.
